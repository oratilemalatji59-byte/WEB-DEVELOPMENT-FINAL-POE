Made for shine, not stickiness

Glaze started in a Johannesburg kitchen in 2024, when our founder got tired of glosses that glued hair to her face. We spent a year testing gel bases until we found one that looks wet and feels weightless.

Today every Glaze tube is made in small batches, with vegan ingredients and no fragrance irritants.
